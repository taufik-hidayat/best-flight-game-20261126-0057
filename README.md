**RIDGELINE COURIER — Alpine Air Mail.** Fly the little orange bush plane *Kestrel* through a chain of glowing rings over a golden-hour alpine valley — Mirror Lake, the pine forest, Millbrook village and the snowy peaks — then bring her home to the runway before the fuel or the clock runs out. A complete 3D flight game in the browser: bank to turn, mind the stall, watch the fuel, and land her gentle.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

**↑ ↓** pitch · **← →** bank & turn · **Shift / Ctrl** throttle · **Space** airbrake · **P** pause · **M** sound — or hold the mouse and drag to steer.

Each ring pays 100 points, +8 seconds and a splash of fuel. Land mid-run to refuel and repair; finish every ring and land on the runway to complete the run, then fly a longer one. The HUD shows altitude, airspeed, heading on a compass tape, vertical speed, an artificial horizon, throttle, fuel, damage, score and the clock.

Built from scratch with Three.js (vendored under `game/vendor/`), procedural terrain, a seeded valley that is identical on every visit, and synthesised WebAudio sound — no audio or image files, no network requests.
